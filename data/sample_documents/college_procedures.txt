Document: Apex University - Standard Administrative & Campus Procedures

1. Student Smart ID Card Issuance & Replacement
- New students receive their RFID Smart ID card during New Student Orientation week at the Student Affairs Center (SAC Block B, Ground Floor).
- Lost/Damaged Cards:
  * Report lost card immediately via student ERP portal to disable RFID building access and e-wallet balance.
  * Submit Replacement Application Form at SAC Counter 3.
  * Fee: $20 replacement charge payable through the student portal.
  * Processing time: 2 business days. Temporary paper pass provided in the interim.

2. Official Transcripts and Bonafide Certificates
- Bonafide Certificate (for passport, visa, education loans, or internships):
  * Apply via Student Portal > Academic Records > Request Certificate.
  * Instant digitally signed e-certificate generated within 15 minutes for valid active students.
- Official Physical Transcripts:
  * Fee: $10 per sealed copy.
  * Processing time: 3 to 5 business days. International courier delivery available for additional postage.

3. Hostel Accommodation and Room Change Procedure
- Hostel check-in requires biometric registration at the Residential Warden Office.
- Room change requests open during the 3rd week of each semester through the Hostel Management Portal. Mutual room swaps between consented students are approved within 48 hours.
- Curfew Hours: Campus main gates and residence halls close at 10:00 PM on weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends. Late entry requires prior approval from the Faculty Warden.

4. Fee Payment & Installment Plans
- Semester tuition and hostel fees must be settled prior to the start of course registration.
- Installment Requests: Students experiencing temporary financial hardship may apply for a 2-part split payment plan via the Bursar Office (Financial Aid Desk) at least two weeks before term start.
- Late Fee: $5 per day late charge applies after the published payment deadline.
